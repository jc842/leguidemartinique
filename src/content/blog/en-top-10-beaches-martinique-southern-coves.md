# Top 10 Beaches in Martinique: From Iconic Coconut Havens to Black Sand Coves

With over 350 kilometers of diverse tropical coastline, Martinique provides some of the most scenic beachscapes in the Lesser Antilles.

---

## 1. Plage des Salines (Sainte-Anne)
The archetype of the Caribbean paradise: over a kilometer of fine golden sand, gently leaning coconut palms, and calm turquoise waters looking out toward Saint Lucia.

## 2. Plage de Grande Anse d'Arlet
A sheltered bay known for calm, mirror-flat water, colorful Creole beach shacks serving grilled mahi-mahi, and sea turtles swimming just meters from the shoreline.

## 3. Anse Dufour & Anse Noire
Twin coves separated by a narrow rocky promontory with contrasting geologies: Anse Dufour features brilliant white coral sand, while Anse Noire displays intense jet-black volcanic sand.

## 4. Anse Céron & Anse Couleuvre (Le Prêcheur)
Deep in the wild north, these untouched black volcanic beaches are sheltered beneath sheer tropical rainforest cliffs.

## 5. Cap Chevalier & Anse Michel
Protected by an offshore barrier reef, this shallow lagoon on the Atlantic side is a world-class destination for kitesurfing, windsurfing, and peaceful swimming.
