# The Martinique Rhum Agricole AOC Trail: Distilleries, Terroir & Tasting

Martinique holds the distinction of producing the world's only *Appellation d'Origine Contrôlée* (AOC) rum, distilled exclusively from 100% fresh pure sugar cane juice (*vesou*).

---

## 1. Iconic Distilleries of Martinique
- **Habitation Clément** (Le François): A blend of world-class contemporary Caribbean sculpture, botanical park, and historic cellars aging vintage rums in oak casks.
- **Distillerie Neisson** (Le Carbet): An artisanal, family-run distillery famous for its organic sugarcane plots and legendary white and aged rums (*Zepol' Karé*).
- **Distillerie Depaz** (Saint-Pierre): Nestled on the fertile volcanic foothills of Mount Pelée, utilizing volcanic spring water for distillation.
- **Saint-James** (Sainte-Marie): Home to the historic Rum Museum and vintage steam-powered plantation train.

## 2. Tasting & The Art of the Ti-Punch
The ritual of the *Ti-Punch* requires three authentic elements:
1. 50° or 55° white AOC Rhum Agricole.
2. A squeeze of fresh Caribbean lime (*citron vert*).
3. A spoonful of unrefined cane sugar syrup (*sirop de batterie*).
