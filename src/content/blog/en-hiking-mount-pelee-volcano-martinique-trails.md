# Hiking Mount Pelée & The Pitons du Carbet: UNESCO Trails & Safety

Designated a UNESCO World Heritage site in 2023, the volcanic complex of Mount Pelée and the Pitons du Carbet constitutes the wild green heart of Martinique.

---

## 1. The Aileron Ascent Trail
- **Starting Point**: Le Premier Refuge parking lot (elevation 824m) near Le Morne-Rouge.
- **Duration**: 4 to 5 hours round-trip.
- **Elevation Gain**: ~600 meters of vertical ascent over volcanic rock steps.
- **Features**: Hiking across the crater rim (*Caldeira*) and reaching the highest dome (*Le Chinois*, 1,397m) with sweeping views of both Atlantic and Caribbean waters on clear mornings.

## 2. Canal des Esclaves (Canal de Beauregard)
A historic aqueduct path clinging to cliff edges above the Carbet river valley, offering a gentle yet thrilling walk surrounded by lush rainforest canopy.

## 3. Preparation & Mountain Safety
- **Early Departure**: Start your ascent before 7:30 AM to beat the mid-morning summit clouds.
- **Gear**: Trail shoes with high traction, waterproof windbreaker, sun protection, and at least 2 liters of hydration.
